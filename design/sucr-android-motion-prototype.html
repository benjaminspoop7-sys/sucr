Sucr for Android — motion graphics

The app's motion language, reviewed before a line of Kotlin compiles. Tokens unchanged from design/tokens.css; patterns and rules from android-ux-skills/references/standards.md.

Motion scale — O-001 / O-003
In-app ui.motion

Android's "Remove animations" writes 0.0f to all three scales and snaps to the end state — it does not soften. Reduced here snaps too, so every state change must survive on its own.

Scheme — T-004 / T-009
Spatial springs

Expressive fast spatial overshoots (dampingRatio .6 / stiffness 800). Watch it on the progress bars and the wizard fill — T-004 keeps it off geometry that encodes a value, which is why the shipped default is Standard.

Interactions worth doing

1. Tap a download row → container transform to the detail screen.
2. Drag that scrims & sheet: open the overflow sheet and flick it — release velocity carries the settle.
3. On the detail screen, press and drag the left edge → predictive back scrubs the exit live.
4. Tap + Add → the FAB morphs into the anchored add bar (anchored, so scaling is allowed).
5. Walk the wizard, then press Back — the reverse must retrace the path.
6. Tap Finish → one hero moment. Then toggle reduced and repeat it.
7. Scroll with Simulate slow transfer on to watch the skeleton's four-step sequence.
