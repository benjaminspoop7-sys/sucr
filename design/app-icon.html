recommended

The creature the app already is, redrawn for the small sizes: three separated arms, a mantle that tapers into them, a full-bleed tile instead of a white card with a hairline. At 16 and 24 a simplified cut drops the suckers and thickens the arms.

One tentacle forming an S, the tail ending in a suction cup. The most legible of the three at 16, and the least like the app it belongs to.

Where it lives

Four places the icon is drawn at a different size, each at that size rather than scaled in the browser.

What shipping it costs

The tray-and-arrow mark was built and relinked on 7 November 2026, then rejected by the owner that same day. The octopus is the shipped icon again; A and B above stay drafts.

The export set

  • One SVG at 1024, the master.
  • PNG at 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 128, 256, 512.
  • One .ico: 16, 24, 32, 48, 64 and 128 as bitmap frames, 256 as PNG.
  • The tray PNGs at 16 and 32.

The product cost

The icon is a Windows resource, so it only lands when the exe is relinked. The tray-and-arrow build measured sucr.exe 4,654,592 B (+60,928). Reverted 7 November 2026: the octopus relinked at sucr.exe 4,591,616 B, 651,264 B under the 5 MB ceiling. No Rust, renderer or IPC code was touched in either build.
