The one thing to keep. XDM's marketing bar is a dashed fill — decoration shaped like data. Ours is a sample of the map: 24 cells cut from the file's block plan, each drawn with the map's own rule — a band's width is that network's share of the span, the remainder is track, and two bands in one cell is a hedge (the same wording as the inspector's legend). The two racing bands are separated by a 1px track notch, because the net hues measure 1.10 against each other in light and 1.03 in dark: hue cannot carry that boundary at any cell size, so the notch does — and the map's own cells need the same notch. The bar then needs 24 × 8 px plus 23 × 2 px gaps = 238 px, exactly the cell floor §4.5 sets; a narrower pane drops to a 16-cell sample (158 px) instead of drawing 6 px cells. The row's bar and the inspector's map become the same object at two sizes, for the cost of one flex container.

Take 2 — solid category badge with a glyph, not a lettered border their blue circles; our category hues

Light glyph 3.0 gate, not 4.5 — it is a mark, not text
Dark glyph flips to ink-dark; hues stay the same family

A 36px square filled with the category hue reads faster in a long list than a bordered box saying MKV, and it removes the one place the list still needed a 4.5 text gate on a saturating colour. The glyph is a graphic, so the gate is 3.0 — white clears it on all four light hues (3.62 on amber to 5.27 on violet, measured on this render); the extension stays in the tooltip and the table's Category column.

The card is the one place a ring earns its keep: it is watched, not read. It stays a solid arc — at 64px the circumference is 176px, so a 24-cell segmented arc would be 7px per cell, under the 8px floor, and an arc cell cannot show two bands at all. The bar underneath carries the per-block information, so nothing is lost. The card's elevation is --e-2 and nothing else: soft shadow in light, 1px ring in dark, one variable.

Neutral selection stops the accent doing two jobs at once — "you are here" and "this is the primary action" — and it is what XDM's site does well. It also buys a contrast rung: on the dark accent tint a --text-3 count measures 4.43, under the 4.5 gate, so it has to climb to --text-2; on --s2 the same --text-3 measures 4.74 and can stay quiet. Keep the accent tint for the list row, where the point is which row the inspector is showing, not where you are in the rail.
